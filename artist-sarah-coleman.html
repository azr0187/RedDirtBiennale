Sarah Coleman

Landscape architecture, Auburn University

Sarah Coleman is a professor in the Landscape Architecture Program at Auburn University. Her research is grounded in an interest in the complexities of layered cultural landscapes.

She focuses on the overlays of past and present that shape shifting understandings and uses of places. Her work examines the paradoxes inherent in how historic cultural landscapes are treated within dominant preservation and heritage paradigms.
