DIRT = HISTORY
POWER = COMMUNITY
POWER = PLACE

The dirt is more than soil, it is a living
Archive, a record of Memory, a record
of Labor, a record of Survival.

The Camp Hill Red Dirt Biennale emerged from the desire of a group of local artists, citizens, students & educators to establish a weekend devoted to local & regional art, music, culture, and history – to transform different locations across Camp Hill into places for reflection, connection and imagination.

The hope was to invite a new way of looking at a space, and to consider how memory, community, and land shape the power we carry.

Power
in Place

How memory, land, identity, and community shape the power we carry and the places we call home.
